# Kiara Vision

Kiara exists to build a frontier-scale reasoning-first foundation model from first principles. It is designed for researchers, engineers, and builders who want to understand and create the core machinery of modern AI rather than rely only on closed APIs.

## Why Kiara exists

Kiara exists to close the gap between high-level AI products and the underlying systems that make them possible. The project is meant to explore how a modern foundation model is built, trained, evaluated, and improved over time.

## Who it is built for

Kiara is built for people interested in:

- large language models
- reasoning systems
- coding and scientific research
- machine learning infrastructure
- open, extensible AI development

## What makes it different

Kiara is not only a model wrapper or a chatbot interface. It is intended to be a full research and engineering platform for building and studying foundation models from the ground up.

## Problems it solves

Kiara aims to address several problems:

- the lack of transparent, extensible model-building infrastructure
- the difficulty of studying the full AI stack beyond API usage
- the need for open, reproducible experimentation
- the gap between research ideas and real training systems

## Where Kiara should be in 5 years

In five years, Kiara should be a mature, evolving AI platform with strong reasoning, coding, and research capabilities, supported by a robust training, evaluation, and alignment stack.
