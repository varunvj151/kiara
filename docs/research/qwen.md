# Qwen

## Architecture
Decoder-only transformer architecture with strong multilingual and coding capabilities.

## Parameter Sizes
Small to large-scale open-weight variants.

## Context Window
Expanding context support across releases.

## Tokenizer
Efficient tokenizer with strong coverage across languages and code.

## Training Strategy (publicly known)
Large-scale multilingual and code-focused pretraining with fine-tuning.

## Attention Mechanism
Transformer attention with practical scaling choices.

## Positional Encoding
RoPE-style or related positional methods.

## Strengths
Strong multilingual support and coding performance.

## Weaknesses
Still dependent on scaling and careful post-training to compete at the frontier.
