## Architecture
Efficient decoder-only transformer architecture focused on performance-per-parameter.

## Parameter Sizes
Smaller and mid-sized open-weight models with strong efficiency.

## Context Window
Practical context windows suitable for many production workloads.

## Tokenizer
Efficient tokenizer with strong compression and inference characteristics.

## Training Strategy (publicly known)
Efficient scaling, careful data curation, and targeted fine-tuning.

## Attention Mechanism
Transformer attention with efficiency-oriented design.

## Positional Encoding
RoPE or similar modern positional mechanisms.

Efficiency and strong serving characteristics.

## Weaknesses
