# Claude

## Architecture
Decoder-only transformer-based language model with strong instruction-following and safety-focused alignment.

## Parameter Sizes
Publicly discussed model sizes vary by release, but the family is designed around large-scale, highly aligned foundation models.

## Context Window
Large context windows are a major strength of modern Claude-family systems.

## Tokenizer
A proprietary tokenizer optimized for long-context language modeling and robust instruction handling.

## Training Strategy (publicly known)
Likely uses large-scale pretraining followed by instruction tuning and preference-based optimization.

## Attention Mechanism
Transformer attention with robust long-context behavior.

## Positional Encoding
Likely uses standard learned or rotary-style positional methods, depending on version.

## Strengths
Strong reasoning, instruction following, and long-context handling.

## Weaknesses
Closed ecosystem and less transparency into training details.

## Research Papers
- Anthropic model and alignment research publications
- Constitutional AI and preference optimization work

## What Kiara can improve
Kiara can emphasize transparency, open experimentation, and a more modular research stack.
