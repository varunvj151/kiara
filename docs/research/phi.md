# Phi

## Architecture
Compact transformer-based model family focused on efficient training and high quality at smaller scales.

## Parameter Sizes
Small to mid-sized models designed for efficient deployment.

## Context Window
Moderate context support with strong practical usability.

## Tokenizer
Efficient tokenizer tuned for compact yet capable language modeling.

## Training Strategy (publicly known)
High-quality data curation, compact model scaling, and targeted post-training.

## Attention Mechanism
Transformer attention with efficient design choices.

## Positional Encoding
Modern positional methods suited to smaller-scale training.

## Strengths
Strong quality-per-parameter and practical deployment.

## Weaknesses
May not match the largest frontier models on the hardest benchmarks.
