# Llama

## Architecture
Decoder-only Transformer.

## Parameter Sizes
Ranges from small to very large open-weight models.

## Context Window
Moderate to large depending on version.

## Tokenizer
BPE-based tokenizer with strong efficiency and broad adoption.

## Training Strategy (publicly known)
Large-scale pretraining on public text corpora with post-training adaptation.

## Attention Mechanism
Grouped Query Attention.

## Positional Encoding
RoPE.

## Strengths
Efficient and widely adopted.

## Weaknesses
Limited reasoning compared to the strongest proprietary frontier models.

## Research Papers
- Llama family papers
- Efficient transformer and training papers

## What Kiara can improve
Kiara can improve reasoning quality, training transparency, and extensibility.
