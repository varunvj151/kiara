# Gemini

## Architecture
Multimodal foundation model architecture designed for language, vision, and cross-modal reasoning.

## Parameter Sizes
Large-scale systems with strong multimodal capabilities.

## Context Window
Very large context windows are a major emphasis.

## Tokenizer
Tokenizer design is optimized for multilingual and multimodal inputs.

## Training Strategy (publicly known)
Large-scale pretraining with multimodal data and post-training alignment.

## Attention Mechanism
Transformer-based attention with scaling and multimodal integration.

## Positional Encoding
Advanced positional and sequence handling mechanisms for long-context tasks.

## Strengths
Strong multimodal reasoning and large-context capabilities.

## Weaknesses
Complex ecosystem and less transparency into internals.

## Research Papers
- Gemini family research
- Multimodal model papers

## What Kiara can improve
Kiara can emphasize a simpler, more transparent stack while still supporting future multimodal expansion.
