# GPT

## Architecture
Decoder-only transformer architecture optimized for general language modeling and broad task performance.

## Parameter Sizes
Ranges from smaller models to very large frontier-scale systems.

## Context Window
Context windows have expanded significantly over successive model generations.

## Tokenizer
Byte-level or subword-based tokenization designed for broad language coverage.

## Training Strategy (publicly known)
Large-scale pretraining on diverse text corpora, followed by instruction tuning and alignment.

## Attention Mechanism
Standard transformer self-attention with scaling and optimization enhancements.

## Positional Encoding
Positional embeddings or related techniques depending on generation.

## Strengths
Versatility, broad knowledge, and strong general-purpose performance.

## Weaknesses
Closed development and high compute requirements.

## Research Papers
- GPT series papers
- Scaling laws and instruction tuning research

Kiara can focus on openness, modularity, and research-oriented transparency.
