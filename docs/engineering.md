# Kiara Engineering Principles

## Principle 1
Every module must be replaceable.

## Principle 2
Everything must be benchmarked.

## Principle 3
No hidden magic.

## Principle 4
Every experiment must be reproducible.

## Principle 5
Every decision must be documented.

## Principle 6
The system should remain modular even as it scales.

## Principle 7
Research progress should be measurable, not assumed.
